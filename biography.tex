\chapter*{Biographical Sketch}
\pdfbookmark[0]{Biographical Sketch}{biosketch}

Zhenyun Xie obtained his B.Sc. in telecommunications engineering in 2017 from the Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV), Spain.
Part of his studies was carried out at the University of Gävle, Sweden, between 2016 and 2017.
He received his M.Sc. degree in telecommunications engineering from Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV) in 2022, where he was awarded the Best Master’s Thesis Award from Cátedra DEXTROMÉDICA.
He is currently a software engineer at iPronics Programmable Photonics S.L., Valencia, Spain, and is also pursuing a Ph.D. in telecommunications at UPV.
His research interests focus on advanced programming methodologies for photonic processors.
